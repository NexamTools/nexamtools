Our Services

Solid Carbide Tools Manufacturing

We manufacture a wide range of high-precision solid carbide cutting tools using premium branded carbide rods for superior performance and tool life.

  • Solid Carbide Drills & End Mills
  • Ball Nose & Bull Nose Endmillss
  • Reamers & Hole mills
  • Special Purpose & Custom Tools

EDM Super DRILL

High-performance EDM super drill for precision deep hole drilling in die & mold, aerospace, and CNC tool room applications.

  • Diameter: Ø0.3 – Ø3.0 mm
  • Depth: Up to 300 mm
  • Electrodes: Brass / Copper / Tungsten
  • Materials: Hardened steel, carbide, alloys

Custom & Special Tool Development

Tools engineered to meet unique component geometries and specific machining requirements.

  • Non-standard tools
  • Profile & form tools
  • Production-optimized solutions
  • Through-coolant capability

Brazed Cutting Tools

High-performance tools with brazed carbide tips for precision machining and long tool life.

  • Solid carbide cutting edges
  • Custom geometries for specific applications
  • High durability and wear resistance
  • Through-coolant and multi-edge options

Tool Regrinding & Resharpening

Maximize tool life and reduce costs while maintaining peak performance.

  • Ensures 80% of the original tool life.
  • Reduced production and tooling costs
  • Restore. Perform. Save.

Coating & Re-coating

Enhance tool performance and longevity with advanced coating and re-coating solutions.

  • Helica, Pertura, Latuma, Aldura, Alcrona, TiCN, TiN, and more.
  • Suitable for all cutting tool geometries.
  • Enhances wear resistance, performance, and tool life.
