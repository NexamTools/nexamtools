Who We Are

Nexam Tools is a trusted manufacturer of high-performance Solid Carbide Cutting Tools, established in 2015 at Coimbatore. We specialize in delivering precision-engineered tooling solutions for modern manufacturing industries.

Our expertise covers the production of standard carbide tools, special tools, and advanced tool design solutions, developed to meet diverse and demanding industrial applications..

Our Story of Growth

Nexam Tools was founded in 2015 by Mr. Sakratees Pugazhenthi, a seasoned technocrat with over 23 years of extensive experience in cutting tool design and manufacturing. His deep technical expertise and industry insight laid the foundation for building a company focused on innovation, quality, and customer-centric solutions.

Since inception, Nexam Tools has grown into a specialist manufacturer of high-performance standard and special carbide cutting tools, offering custom-designed solutions for demanding industrial applications across diverse sectors.

Our journey continues with a clear focus on technological advancement, quality excellence, and ethical growth enabling us to remain a dependable partner for precision tooling solutions.
