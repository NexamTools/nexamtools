Welcome to Nexam Tools

Nexam Tools, established in 2015 at Coimbatore, is a specialist manufacturer of high-performance Solid Carbide Cutting Tools.

Founded by Mr. Sakratees Pugazhenthi, a technocrat with over 23 years of expertise in cutting tool design and manufacturing.

We manufacture standard and special carbide tools, delivering custom-designed solutions to meet demanding industrial applications.
